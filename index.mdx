AGRO-JUTIAPA is an open-access platform that combines 45 years of ERA5 satellite climate reanalysis, FAO-56 soil water balance physics, and calibrated XGBoost machine learning to give farmers in the Guatemalan Dry Corridor a data-driven sowing viability verdict — no login or account required.

## What AGRO-JUTIAPA Does

Every time a farmer submits a planting date and parcel dimensions, the platform:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Downloads live climate data">
    Fetches daily precipitation, reference evapotranspiration (ET₀), and mean temperature from the Open-Meteo ERA5 Archive API (1981 – present), plus ENSO indices (MEI v2, Niño 3.4) from NOAA PSL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Simulates soil water balance">
    Runs a continuous FAO-56 bucket model using Saxton & Rawls pedotransfer equations calibrated for Jutiapa's soil profile (26% clay, 52% sand, 1.8% organic matter).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Infers viability probability">
    Applies a calibrated XGBoost / Logistic Regression ensemble (temporal GroupKFold CV, Brier Skill Score validated) to return a probability score from 0 to 100%.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delivers a tri-color verdict">
    Maps the probability to a Green / Yellow / Red traffic light, a phenological rain streak analysis across 120 crop days, and a full P&L financial projection using real-time MAGA wholesale prices.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key Features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="ERA5 Climate Reanalysis" icon="cloud-sun-rain">
    45 years of daily satellite reanalysis data with automatic local caching and exponential-backoff retries.
  </Card>
  <Card title="FAO-56 Soil Balance" icon="droplet">
    Continuous bucket simulation tracking soil moisture fraction, water stress coefficient Kₛ, and waterlogging days.
  </Card>
  <Card title="XGBoost + Logit Ensemble" icon="brain">
    Four model configurations compared via AUC and Brier Skill Score; best model selected and sigmoid-calibrated automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Traffic Light System" icon="traffic-light">
    Green ≥ 60%, Yellow 35–59%, Red &lt; 35% — with ENSO phase context and actionable recommendations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="120-Day Fenology Analysis" icon="chart-line">
    Stage-by-stage rain streak analysis (germination → vegetative → flowering → grain fill) with canícula detection.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Real-Time MAGA Prices" icon="chart-bar">
    Live wholesale maize price scraper from Guatemala's official MAGA market portal for accurate P&L projections.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Terrain Geometry" icon="vector-square">
    CAD-style area calculation for rectangular, triangular, and irregular quadrilateral parcels in m², manzanas, and hectares.
  </Card>
  <Card title="PDF Report Export" icon="file-pdf">
    Three-page official technical dictum with water balance charts, terrain layout, and financial summary.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  AGRO-JUTIAPA is calibrated exclusively for **Calderas, San José Acatempa, Jutiapa, Guatemala** (Lat 14.240–14.290, Lon −90.160–−90.110). Requests outside this bounding box are rejected.
</Note>
